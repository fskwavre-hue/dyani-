4. Personal data

What this site collects

Nothing as a result of your visit. This site is made of static pages. It has no user accounts, no database and no analytics. Only sending the contact form transmits information; that is the subject of the paragraph below.

The contact form

Until you click a send button, what you type stays in your browser and never leaves your device.

Two routes are offered. The WhatsApp button simply opens your application with the message already written: you alone decide whether to send it.

The Send by e-mail button submits your message to our mailbox through the Web3Forms service, which relays it to us. The details you have filled in — name, e-mail address, establishment, city, headcount and the content of the message — therefore pass through that provider, for that relay alone. Web3Forms sets no cookie on your device; its privacy policy is available at web3forms.com.

The information in your message is kept for as long as it takes to handle your enquiry and the commercial follow-up that may result, then deleted. It is never sold or passed on to third parties.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics tool, no advertising tracker and no social media button. That is why you are shown no consent banner: there is nothing to consent to.

The site stores one single piece of information on your device: the language you chose, so it can be offered again. It stays in your browser, is never sent to us, and disappears if you clear the site's data.

External resources

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. In doing so, your IP address is sent to Google, which is solely responsible for it. This is the only external resource this site calls.

Your rights

Under Moroccan law 09-08 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data, and under the General Data Protection Regulation for visitors resident in the European Union, you have the right to access, correct, object to and delete data concerning you.

As this site records nothing from your visit, these rights can only concern the content of a message you may have sent us. To exercise them, simply write to the address given in the contact section.

In Morocco, the competent authority is the Commission nationale de contrôle de la protection des données à caractère personnel (CNDP).
